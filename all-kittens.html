Adoption Status Guide Please read this guide to understand what each status means. ▾
  • Coming Soon — Not ready for adoption. These kittens are still very young and are growing, developing their personalities, and learning through their time in foster care.
  • Almost Available — Getting close to being ready for adoption, but not currently available. Check back later for updates.
  • Available — Currently available for adoption.
  • Pending Interest — An adoption application is currently in progress. The applicant may be completing the adoption process, waiting for a meet-and-greet, or completing another step with the rescue. Other applicants may still apply. The rescue works on a first-come, first-served basis. If the current application does not move forward, another applicant may have the opportunity to continue the adoption process.
  • Adopted — Adoption complete — this kitten has found a forever home.
  • Returned — Available — These cats or kittens were previously adopted and are now available for adoption again. They may need additional patience and time as they adjust back to foster care.

Important: Adoption applications are accepted when a kitten has been spayed or neutered, completed the necessary veterinary care, been cleared by the rescue, and is officially listed as Available.
