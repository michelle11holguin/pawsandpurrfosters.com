Adoption Status Guide

Please read this guide to understand what each status means.

  • Available — Currently available for adoption. Adoption interests and applications are being accepted.
  • Returned — Available — Previously adopted and now looking for a new home. These kittens may need additional patience and time to settle into a new home.
  • Pending Interest — Someone is currently interested or going through the adoption process. Other people may still apply. We work on a first-come, first-served basis, and if the current application does not move forward, another approved applicant may have the opportunity to adopt.
  • Almost Available — Getting close to being ready for adoption, but not currently available.
  • Pre-Adopted — A specific person has expressed interest and an adoption is being arranged. This is a rare status and does not mean the adoption is finalized.
  • Coming Soon — Not ready for adoption yet. These kittens may still be growing, receiving medical care, developing their personalities, or getting comfortable in foster care.
  • Adopted — Adoption has been completed. Adopted kittens will eventually be moved to the Foster Alumni section.

Important: “Coming Soon” and “Almost Available” do not mean a kitten is currently available for adoption. Adoption applications are accepted only when a kitten has been medically cleared and is officially listed as Available.
