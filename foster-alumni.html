Our Foster Alumni

All these kittens have graduated from foster care and found their forever homes. Once our fosters, always in our hearts. Celebrate the kittens who have found their forever families and will always hold a special place in our story.

Alumni Badge Guide Learn what our alumni badges mean. ▾
  • Foster Fail — These kittens started out in our foster care, but along the way, we fell in love and decided to make them permanent members of our family. Sometimes, a foster kitten just finds their forever home right where they started!
  • Second Chance — These kittens were adopted, returned to foster care, and later found their forever homes again. Their stories are a reminder that every kitten deserves a second chance.
