About Paws & Purr Fosters

Helping little paws find their forever homes.

Paws & Purr Fosters is a small foster team run by my brother and me. We have been fostering kittens since February 29, 2024, and are now on our 12th litter.

Every kitten receives plenty of love, care, socialization, and positive experiences while waiting for a forever home. Most of our kittens are also harness-trained to help them build confidence and enjoy new experiences.
